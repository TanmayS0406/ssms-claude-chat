What can SSMS Claude Chat do?

It works with whatever server and database your query window is connected to.

🔎

Answers questions about your database

"Which tables hold customer data?" "What uses this column?" Claude looks it up and tells you.

🧩

Explains and writes stored procedures

Get any procedure explained in plain English, or describe what you need and Claude writes it for you.

⚡

Helps fix slow queries

Paste a query or attach an execution plan, and Claude explains what's slow and how to fix it.

🗺️

Draws diagrams

Database (ER) diagrams and flowcharts of procedures in one click, even across several databases.

✋

You stay in control

Before anything changes, you see exactly what will run and where. Nothing happens until you click Yes.

💬

Remembers your chats

Attach files and screenshots, and pick up any earlier conversation where you left off.
